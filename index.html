How it would run

Next to your stack, not instead of it. And past the paywall too.

One question for you: how fast can the app test?

Today every paywall or offer change in the app ships with an app release. How many variants did you get live in the app last quarter, and how many on the web? In Flow Builder, every screen on this page goes live and into an A/B test without a release.

It plugs into your stack

Adapty sends subscription events into mParticle and Braze, and runs alongside Adjust. It doesn’t replace anything: your CDP, your CRM and your attribution stay as they are, and the app’s paywalls get the speed your web and email already have.

After the paywall: cancel flows and win-back

The same builder runs the moment a supporter cancels (pause, the six-month plan, a reason to stay) and the win-back offer for lapsed supporters.

56%of subscribers who came back on a win-back offer are still paying three months later

Benchmarks across categories

Adapty’s State of Paywalls has no news category, so the numbers on this page are cross-category: $170 vs $40 per 1,000 views for onboarding vs later paywalls, 40% of buyers needing more than one view, 4% of apps with a second paywall, 2% with a personalised headline.

Later: Feast and Guardian Puzzles. Both could run on the same platform and SDK once the news app is live. A future option, not part of this test.
